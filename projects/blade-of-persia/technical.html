Context & my role

I serve as both Game Director and Technical Director on Universal Power Blade of Persia. My work combines hands-on gameplay programming with creative direction, story and quest writing, and coordination of voice actors. I developed and released the Steam demo during 2023-2025; development of the full game continues.

What I personally built

Core gameplay systems

I personally designed and implemented the gameplay systems supporting the project, alongside the game direction and technical direction needed to bring the demo to release.

Story and quests

I wrote the storyline and questline, taking responsibility for the narrative as well as the technical work.

Voice production

I managed voice actors during production and coordinated their contributions to the game.

How I built it

I combined the responsibilities of directing the game and implementing its systems in Unreal Engine. This meant working across gameplay development, narrative work, and production coordination.

I took the project through a playable demo release on Steam, giving players a public build to experience while development of the full game continued.

Results & impact

Released a playable Steam demo and received positive player feedback. The demo is a public example of the project; the full game remains in development.
