Context & my role

I’m the solo Game Developer on Universal Power: Blade of Persia. My work combines gameplay programming, story and quest writing, and production work. I developed and released the Steam demo during 2023-2025; development of the full game continues.

What I personally built

Core gameplay systems

I personally designed and implemented the gameplay systems supporting the project, taking the project through to its public Steam demo.

Story and quests

I wrote the storyline and questline, taking responsibility for the narrative as well as the technical work.

Voice production

I managed voice actors during production and coordinated their contributions to the game.

How I built it

As the project’s Game Developer, I implemented its systems in Unreal Engine. This meant working across gameplay development, narrative work, and production coordination.

I took the project through a playable demo release on Steam, giving players a public build to experience while development of the full game continued.

Results & impact

Released a playable Steam demo and received positive player feedback. The demo is a public example of the project; the full game remains in development.
