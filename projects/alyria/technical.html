Context & my role

As Technical Director at Iron Pillar Studios, I joined a project that needed gameplay stabilization, performance improvements, and a stronger technical foundation. My work involved hands-on system development alongside coordinating the technical team on limited funding.

What I personally built

Combat and gameplay

I personally designed and implemented core gameplay systems, including combat, movement, crafting, inventory, minigames, elixir use and throwing, quests, and procedural content generation (PCG).

Reactive and emotion-driven AI

I personally built an AI scheduling and task system that responds to player movement, character state, and NPC health. An emotion-driven layer uses fear, panic, anger, and confidence to influence attacking, retreating, and self-defense. Reactions account for witnessed or reported harm to friends, family, and civilians.

Narrative

I wrote the storyline and questline for Alyria.

Technical leadership

I directed development work and helped bring the project from an unstable starting point to working, connected gameplay.

How I built it

The combat system was rebuilt in approximately one week after I joined. The wider work focused on making the core systems usable together and supporting ongoing development.

I used modular systems to support iteration and reuse. I used asynchronous loading and unloading to reduce memory use and profiled CPU and GPU work to improve performance.

The AI combines scheduled tasks with reactions to current gameplay conditions. Emotional state changes behavior: an NPC may retreat when afraid or defend itself when attacked, while remembered or reported harm affects its response to the player.

The project required balancing technical work with team coordination and retaining momentum despite limited funding.

Results & impact

Led an eight-month turnaround that delivered working combat, quests, movement, crafting, inventory, minigames, elixir mechanics, and PCG systems. Reduced memory usage from 4.3 GB to 1.8 GB, approximately 58%. Raised average frame rate from 97 to 159 FPS, approximately 64%, through profiling and optimization. These are the before-and-after results from my work on Alyria.
