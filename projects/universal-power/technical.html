Context & my role

Universal Power is a completed game that I developed through release during 2021-2023 and maintained afterward. My involvement covered hands-on programming, story and quest writing, release work, and responding to player issues.

What I personally built

Core gameplay

I personally designed and implemented the core gameplay systems and mechanics needed for the complete game.

Narrative

I wrote the storyline and questline.

Post-release development

I delivered updates and fixes for issues reported by players after release.

How I built it

I took the project from its core-system development through a complete public release. After launch, I used player-reported issues to guide fixes and continued updating the game.

This included maintaining a released game as players encountered it outside development.

Results & impact

Shipped the complete game on Steam and delivered post-release updates and fixes. The store page provides a public reference for the released project.
